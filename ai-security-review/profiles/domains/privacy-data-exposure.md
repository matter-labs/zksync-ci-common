# Privacy and data exposure

Trace sensitive data from its source to responses, events, exports, logs, traces, metrics, caches, persistent storage,
AI prompts, and third-party services. Check excessive fields, missing redaction, audience confusion, shared cache or
storage keys, correlation and enumeration signals, and retention or deletion changes that leave data recoverable.

A finding must identify the sensitive data, the repository evidence that establishes its sensitivity, the unintended
audience or sink, the changed data flow, and a concrete confidentiality impact. Do not report legal or policy compliance
concerns, generic data-minimization advice, or speculative exposure without a reachable disclosure path.

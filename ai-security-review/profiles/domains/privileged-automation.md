# Privileged automation and operator tooling

Check actor and approval gates, target environment and chain selection, command and expression injection, credential
scope, transaction simulation and post-condition checks, idempotency, retry and replay behavior, partial failure,
rollback safety, audit evidence, and the boundary between advisory output and executed action.

A finding must identify an attainable trigger that makes the automation perform an unauthorized or incorrectly targeted
privileged action. Do not report missing confirmations when an equivalent enforced approval gate exists.

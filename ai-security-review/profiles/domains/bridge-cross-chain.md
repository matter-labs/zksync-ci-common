# Bridges and cross-chain messaging

Trace messages and assets from origin through finalization. Check chain and domain binding, sender provenance, finality,
replay protection, message-root and proof verification, canonical asset mapping, mint and burn accounting, deposit and
withdrawal state, refunds, retries, and partial failure across chains and off-chain relayers.

A finding must identify the forged, replayed, duplicated, blocked, or misaccounted message or asset flow. Do not report
generic bridge risk without a changed validation or accounting path.

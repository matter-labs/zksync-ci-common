# Zero-knowledge circuits and provers

Check for under-constrained witnesses, missing range or boolean constraints, host/circuit disagreement, inconsistent
serialization, unchecked public inputs, transcript and domain-separation mistakes, field conversion errors, unsound
optimization, and prover/verifier disagreement.

A finding must identify the missing or inconsistent constraint and explain how an invalid statement or witness could be
accepted. Performance observations are not findings unless they enable a realistic availability attack.

# Authorization

Trace the protected operation from entry point to policy decision and data access. Confirm deny-by-default behavior,
identity and resource binding, organization or actor scoping, role and permission semantics, delegation, cache keys, and
enforcement at every bypassable entry point.

A finding must name the principal, protected capability or data, missing or confused decision, and unauthorized result.
Do not assume an endpoint is private merely because adjacent endpoints are private.
